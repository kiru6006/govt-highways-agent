## 1. Statutory Committees Register

### 1. Board of Engineers (BOE)
- **Governing Orders**: G.O.Ms.No.359, Highways (HN1), dated 12.08.1997; G.O.Ms.No.1, Highways and Minor Ports (HN1), dated 07.01.2011.
- **Chairperson**: Director General (Highways), Highways Department.
- **Composition**: Director General (H) (Chair); Chief Engineer (C&M); Chief Engineer (National Highways); Chief Engineer (Planning, Designs & Investigation); Director (Highways Research Station); Chief Engineer (Projects); Chief Engineer (NABARD & Rural Roads); Chief Engineer (Metro); Chief Engineer (TNRSP-II); Chief Engineer (CKICP).
- **Statutory Mandate**: Highest technical authority of the department. Approves all state engineering techniques, amendments to specifications, manual revisions, and standard drawings before formal government notification.

### 2. Technical Audit Committee (TAC)
- **Governing Orders**: CE(H) General Circular Memo No. 13555/Contracts-1/2009 dated 02.05.2009.
- **Chairperson**: Director General (Highways).
- **Mandatory Review Triggers**:
  - All bridge works with linear waterway &gt; 150 metres.
  - Major multi-lane bypasses.
  - Grade separators and multi-level flyovers.
  - Major Railway Over Bridges (ROB) and Railway Under Bridges (RUB).
- **Legal Function**: Scrutinizes structural designs, foundation options, geotechnical borelogs, and price estimates prior to the accord of Technical Sanction (TS) by the competent Chief Engineer.

### 3. Revised Administrative Sanction Committee (RASC)
- **Governing Orders**: G.O.Ms.No.289 dated 04.12.2008; G.O.Ms.No.1 dated 07.01.2011.
- **Composition**: Director General (H) (Chair); Concerned Chief Engineer; Chief Engineer (PDI); Deputy Secretary to Government (Highways); Deputy Secretary to Government (Finance).
- **Function**: Scrutinizes work packages whose actual cost during execution exceeds the original Administrative Sanction (AS) by more than 10% or due to scope alterations, prior to recommending Revised Administrative Sanction to the Government.

### 4. RASC for Land Acquisition (RASC-LA)
- **Governing Orders**: G.O.Ms.No.163, Highways and Minor Ports (HN1), dated 13.12.2023.
- **Composition**: Commissioner of Land Acquisition (Convener); Additional Chief Secretary to Government, Highways and Minor Ports; Finance Secretary or designated nominee; Concerned Chief Engineer; Representative of Legal Affairs Secretary.
- **Function**: Examines enhanced land acquisition compensation claims, statutory solatium adjustments under the RFCTLARR Act 2013 / TN Highways Act 2001, and court-directed enhancement awards.

### 5. Commissionerate of Tenders (COT)
- **Governing Orders**: G.O.Ms.No.21 (25.01.1999); G.O.Ms.No.16 (11.02.2013); G.O.Ms.No.1 (07.01.2011); G.O.Ms.No.75 (02.09.2026).
- **Composition**: Secretary to Government, Finance (or designated Special Secretary); Director General (Highways) as Commissioner and Member; Chief Engineer (C&M Chennai); Chief Engineer (National Highways). Recommending CEs and SEs attend as non-voting invitees.
- **Statutory Threshold Matrix & Analysis**:

| Tender Value Tier | Tender Excess / Condition | Approving Authority | Mandatory Procedure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Up to ₹50 Lakhs** | Tender excess &gt; 10% | Commissionerate of Tenders (COT) | Requisite justification on SSR rates |
| **₹50 Lakhs to ₹1.00 Crore** | Tender excess &gt; 6% | Commissionerate of Tenders (COT) | Requisite justification on market price |
| **₹1.00 Crore to ₹6.00 Crore** | Any excess (&gt;0%) on individual works | Commissionerate of Tenders (COT) | Tender Evaluation Committee scrutiny |
| **₹1.00 Crore to ₹7.50 Crore** | Any excess (&gt;0%) on package works | Commissionerate of Tenders (COT) | Package consolidation scrutiny |
| **Above ₹6.00 Crore (Individual)** | **All cases irrespective of tender excess or discount** | Commissionerate of Tenders (COT) | Mandatory COT review prior to acceptance |
| **Above ₹7.50 Crore (Packages)** | **All cases irrespective of tender excess or discount** | Commissionerate of Tenders (COT) | Mandatory COT review prior to acceptance |

> [!NOTE]
> **Threshold Ambiguity Resolution**:
> The apparent overlap in the ₹1.00 Cr to ₹6.00 Cr range is resolved by the departmental rule: If an individual work is under ₹6.00 Cr and tendered **at or below estimate rate (0% or negative excess)**, the Chief Engineer can accept it under regular delegated powers. If it has **any excess**, it must go to COT. Above ₹6.00 Cr, all works go to COT regardless of rate.

### 6. Standard Schedule of Rates (SSR) Committee
- **Governing Orders**: G.O.Ms.No.21 dated 25.01.1999; G.O.Ms.No.1 dated 07.01.2011.
- **Composition**: Director General (H) (Chair); Chief Engineer (C&M); Chief Engineer (PDI); Finance Department representative.
- **Function**: Annual formulation of Basic Schedule of Rates for labor, plant, machinery, cement, structural steel, and bitumen (VG-30/VG-40 and CRMB).

### 7. High Level Dispute Resolution Committee (HLDRC)
- **Governing Orders**: G.O.Ms.No.17 dated 06.03.2023; G.O.Ms.No.87 dated 21.07.2023; G.O.(Rt) No.60 dated 20.03.2023; G.O.(Rt) No.209 dated 25.08.2023.
- **Composition**: Director General (H) (Chair); Concerned Chief Engineer (Convener); Additional Chief Secretary to Government (Finance); Project Director (TNRSP-II); Director (HRS); Additional Chief Secretary to Government (Law).
- **Function**: Institutional alternative dispute resolution mechanism to amicably settle contractor disputes, time extension claims, price escalation disputes, and avoid prolonged High Court litigation.

### 8. Three Member Expert Committee under Section 16(b) of TNTIT Act
- **Governing Orders**: G.O.Ms.No.27 dated 10.02.2011; G.O.Ms.No.158 dated 22.08.2022 (disbandment); G.O.Ms.No.05 dated 13.01.2026 (reconstitution).
- **Composition**: Joint Director, Office of the DG (H) (Convener); Concerned Chief Engineer; Chief General Manager (TNRDC); Director (Highways Research Station).
- **Mandate**: Mandatory scrutiny under Section 16(b) of the Tamil Nadu Transparency in Tenders Act 1998 for single-source entrustment of additional quantities exceeding 25% of agreement quantities or new non-tendered items to the existing contractor.
- **Statutory Workflow**:
  $$\text{Field Division Estimate} \longrightarrow \text{Expert Committee Evaluation} \longrightarrow \text{RASC Review} \longrightarrow \text{Government Administrative Sanction}$$

## 2. Tamil Nadu Road Infrastructure Development Corporation (TNRIDC) Governance

TNRIDC is an autonomous government company executing designated mega industrial road projects.

### Board of Directors:
- **Chairman & Managing Director (CMD)**: Principal Secretary to Government, Highways and Minor Ports Department.
- **Directors**:
  - Special Secretary to Government, Finance Department.
  - Managing Director, Tamil Nadu Water Supply and Drainage (TWAD) Board.
  - Executive Director, State Industries Promotion Corporation of Tamil Nadu (SIPCOT).
  - Deputy Secretary to Government, Energy Department.
  - Director General (Highways), Highways Department.
  - Chief Engineer (H), NABARD & Rural Roads.
  - Executive Director, TNRIDC.

### Standing Governance Committees in TNRIDC:
1. **Audit Committee**: Deputy Secretary (Finance), Director General (Highways), Chief Engineer (NABARD), Executive Director (TNRIDC).
2. **Technical Sanction Committee**: Director General (Highways), Chief Engineer (PDI), Director (HRS), Executive Director (TNRIDC).
