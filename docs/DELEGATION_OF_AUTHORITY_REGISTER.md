## 1. Statutory Delegations of Authority

The Tamil Nadu Financial Code and Highways Manual strictly partition authority across:
1. **Administrative Sanction (AS)**: Issued exclusively by the Government (Secretariat / Council of Ministers).
2. **Technical Sanction (TS)**: Accorded by competent engineering cadres based on detailed designs, estimates, and geotechnical surveys.
3. **Tender Acceptance (TA)**: Accorded by competent officers or the Commissionerate of Tenders (COT) based on monetary value and tender excess percentage.

## 2. Decision Enforcement Rules in TN-HDOS

1. **No Designation-Only Inferences**: A user cannot approve a ₹10.00 Cr tender simply because their designation is "Chief Engineer". If the tender contains a 4% excess, the system blocks direct acceptance and generates a statutory referral dossier to the **Commissionerate of Tenders (COT)**.
2. **Additional Charge Restrictions**: An officer holding Additional Charge (`A/C`) of a circle or division cannot sanction non-routine budget re-appropriations without concurrence from the regular substantive supervisory officer or Director General (H).
