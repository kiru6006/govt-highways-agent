# Tamil Nadu Highways Department: External Stakeholders & Inter-Departmental Register

**Purpose**: Formal cataloguing of coordinating government agencies, utility boards, and statutory external bodies  
**System Rule**: External organisations are linked to projects strictly when documented statutory approvals, utility shifting, or legal proceedings exist.

## 2. External Project Entities (Contractors & Consultants)

Contractors and consultants are external entities participating under binding contracts (FIDIC / EPC / Standard PWD):
- **Contractors**: L&T Construction, PST Engineering Construction, KNR Constructions, Transstroy, SPK and Co.
- **Consultants**: STUP Consultants, Feedback Infra, Egis India, Lea Associates, Aarvee Associates.
- **Account Boundary Enforcement**:
  - Scoped strictly to awarded contract packages.
  - Zero access to internal departmental disciplinary inquiries, vigilance flags, or peer contractor evaluations.
