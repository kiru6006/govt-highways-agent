# TN-HDOS Group Messaging & Working Group Operational Rules

## 1. Official Working Group Categories

Government working groups in TN-HDOS must belong to one of 10 codified administrative categories:

1. `PROJECT_WORKING_GROUP`: Dedicated to active road, bridge, and bypass packages (e.g. Chengalpattu-Kanchipuram 4-laning).
2. `DEPARTMENT_WORKING_GROUP`: Intra-departmental operational taskforces (e.g. Highways Design & Research Station, Planning wing).
3. `PROGRAMME_REVIEW`: Externally aided and central programme review boards (e.g. CKICP ADB Tranche 2, TNRSP-II World Bank).
4. `CIRCLE_DIVISION`: Field administrative coordination spanning Superintending Engineer circles and Divisional Engineer offices.
5. `CONTRACTOR_COORDINATION`: Formal coordination forums uniting departmental engineers, project management consultants (PMC), and EPC contractors.
6. `LAND_ACQUISITION`: Joint taskforces including Revenue DROs, Tahsildars, and Highways Special Land Acquisition Officers.
7. `UTILITY_SHIFTING`: Multi-agency synchronization bodies including TANGEDCO (power), TWAD (water), and telecommunication operators.
8. `QUALITY_ASSURANCE`: Specialized quality review clusters handling non-conformance reports, core compressive strength tests, and bitumen quality audits.
9. `LEGAL_DISPUTE`: Sub-judice deliberations, arbitration review cells, and counter-affidavit preparation taskforces with Government Pleaders.
10. `COMMITTEE_REVIEW`: High-level empowered committees, Cabinet sub-committees, and Chief Secretary monitoring boards.

---

## 2. Participant Governance & Member Roles

Participants in any discussion group are tagged with one of four formal conversation roles:
- `CREATOR`: The initiating official with administrative privileges to add/remove members and archive the thread.
- `ADMINISTRATOR`: Superintending Engineers, Chief Engineers, or Project Directors authorized to manage member rosters.
- `MEMBER`: Authorized department engineers or inter-department nodal officers with full reading, posting, and attachment rights.
- `OBSERVER`: Read-only participants (e.g. vigilance inspectors, audit officers, or legal advisors) who monitor progress without conversational interruption.

---

## 3. Communication Conduct & Message Lifecycle

- **Priority Hierarchy**:
  - `ROUTINE`: Standard operational updates, site logs, and daily progress remarks.
  - `MEDIUM`: Requests for clarification, test report submissions, and routine meeting notes.
  - `HIGH`: Imminent milestone deadlines, pending joint inspections, and utility shutdown windows.
  - `CRITICAL`: Urgent ministerial directives, safety breach notifications, court stay alerts, and work-stop orders.
- **Message Edit & Deletion Protocol**:
  - Message editing is permitted within 15 minutes of transmission and flags the message as `is_edited=true`.
  - Hard deletions are forbidden by government records policy. Deleted messages are marked `is_deleted=true` and their full text retained in `ConversationAuditLog` for statutory transparency.
