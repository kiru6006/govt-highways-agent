## 1. Executive Summary & Statutory Charter

The **Highways and Minor Ports Department** of the Government of Tamil Nadu is the premier sovereign agency responsible for planning, designing, constructing, maintaining, and administering the road and bridge network across the State of Tamil Nadu, as well as overseeing non-major/minor ports through the Tamil Nadu Maritime Board (TNMB).

### Core Departmental Mandate & Policy Objectives:
1. **Preservation & Upgradation**: Administering and maintaining 73,187 km of State Highways (SH), Major District Roads (MDR), Other District Roads (ODR), and Special Agricultural/Rural Corridors.
2. **National Highway Stewardship**: Formally entrusted by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Government of India, with improvement and maintenance of 797.562 km of National Highways across 11 key stretches through the State National Highways Wing.
3. **Core Road Network Modernization**: Execution of major multilateral and state-funded infrastructure programs (CMRDP, CRIDP, TNRSP-II World Bank loan assistance, CKICP ADB assistance, and CPRR JICA funding).
4. **Institutional Quality & Research**: Technical standardization, material testing, pavement design, and forensic structural audits spearheaded by the **Highways Research Station (HRS)**, established in 1957.
5. **Road Safety & Accident Reduction**: Institutionalized traffic engineering fixes, blackspot rectifications, pedestrian grade separators, and crash barrier deployment overseen by the Institutional Strengthening and Road Safety (ICERS) Cell under the Director General (H).

## 3. Statutory Cadre Rules Architecture (TN-HES & TN-HESS)

In strict accordance with Tamil Nadu service jurisprudence:
- **G.O.Ms.No.172, Highways and Minor Ports Department, dated 03.11.2025** established the revised Special Rules for the **Tamil Nadu Highways Engineering Service (TN-HES)**.
- **Divisional Engineer (H)** is the statutory cadre designation for division heads. The title "Executive Engineer" is a central/PWD designation and has **no statutory existence** in the State Highways cadre.
- The hierarchy comprises:
  - **Category 1**: Director General (H) / Chief Engineer (H)
  - **Category 2**: Superintending Engineer (H)
  - **Category 3**: Divisional Engineer (H)
  - **Category 4**: Assistant Divisional Engineer (H)
  - **Category 5**: Assistant Engineer (H) (Direct recruit degree engineers under TN-HESS)
- Subordinate and ministerial posts (Junior Engineer [Diploma], Draughting Officers, Chief Accounts Officers, Administrative Officers) belong to **TN-HESS** and the **Tamil Nadu General Service** respectively.
