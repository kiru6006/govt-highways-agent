# Tamil Nadu Highways Department: Organisation Hierarchy & Reorganisation Analysis

**Primary Reference**: G.O.Ms.No.43, Highways and Minor Ports Department, dated 13.03.2026  
**Comparative Reference**: Pre-restructuring Departmental Manual & Policy Notes 2024-25 / 2025-26  
**Status**: BENCHMARKED WITH REORGANISATION ANOMALY FLAGS

---

## 1. Pre-Restructuring Legacy Structure vs. G.O.Ms.No.43 Reorganisation

Prior to the 2026 reorganisation, the department operated with fragmented functional wings:
- **Construction & Maintenance (C&M)** (Divided regionally without consolidated zonal reporting)
- **NABARD & Rural Roads Wing** (Maintained separate circles and divisions for village link roads)
- **Projects Wing** (Maintained independent divisions for bypasses and grade separators)
- **Metropolitan Wing** (Chennai metropolitan specific infrastructure)
- **National Highways Wing** (MoRTH entrusted corridors)
- **Planning, Designs and Investigation (PDI)**
- **Highways Research Station (HRS)**

### The G.O.Ms.No.43 Restructuring Model:
G.O.Ms.No.43 (dated 13.03.2026) consolidated fragmented district infrastructure under **Four Integrated Zonal Commands**, integrating C&M, NABARD & Rural Roads, and selected Projects units under unified Zonal Chief Engineers.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                            STATEWIDE METRICS                                │
│   • 4 Zonal Commands: Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli         │
│   • 19 Engineering Circles (headed by Superintending Engineers)             │
│   • 87 Field Divisions (headed by Divisional Engineers)                     │
│   • 353 Subdivisions (headed by Assistant Divisional Engineers)             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. Specialized Statewide Technical Wings & SPVs

Alongside the 4 C&M Zones, the Director General (H) directly coordinates specialized wings:

1. **National Highways Wing (NH)**:
   - Head: **Er. K. Vanathi, Chief Engineer (H)**
   - Mandate: 797.562 km of 11 MoRTH-entrusted National Highway corridors in Tamil Nadu.
2. **Planning, Designs & Investigation Wing (PDI)**:
   - Head: **Er. S. Yamini, Chief Engineer (H)**
   - Mandate: Alignment surveys, DPR preparation, hydrological modeling, computer-aided structural designs.
3. **Highways Research Station (HRS Guindy)**:
   - Head: **Er. A. Venkatachalam, Director (H)** (Rank of Chief Engineer)

   - **TANSHA**: Er. D. Jebaselwin Gladson (Member Technical) — Tamil Nadu State Highways Authority.

## 4. Reorganisation Date Conflicts & Analysis

> [!WARNING]
> **Data Conflict Notice Regarding G.O.Ms.No.43 Implementation Date**:
> - Government announcements and press briefings indicate restructuring was notified on **13.03.2026**.
> - Some secondary circulars reference transition dates starting from **01.04.2026** (Financial Year 2026-27).
> - In TN-HDOS, the organisation model is built with **effective-dating** (`effective_from` and `effective_to`) so that legacy circle/division records for pre-2026 projects are preserved without anachronistic re-mapping.
