# TN-HDOS AI Governance and Evaluation Report

## 1. Executive Summary & Governance Charter
This report details the formal AI governance evaluation for the **Tamil Nadu Highways Digital Operating System (TN-HDOS)**, conducted under the mandate of the Minister for Highways & Minor Ports, Additional Chief Secretary, and Director General (Highways).

### Core Governance Doctrine
> **"AI recommendations within TN-HDOS are strictly advisory and investigative triggers. Artificial Intelligence shall never possess autonomous statutory authority, automatic sanctioning powers, financial debit execution capability, or punitive action execution capability."**

All AI-generated insights, anomalies, risk indices, and briefings must adhere to:
1. **Explainability**: Clear mathematical and provenance tracking for every score.
2. **Deterministic Evidence Citation**: Every insight cites verified source records (MB entries, lab reports, e-challans, muster rolls, treasury debits).
3. **Audit Immutability**: All prompts, inferences, confidence scores, and user overrides logged to write-once append-only audit tables.
4. **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop**: Statutory proceedings (suspension, blacklisting, recovery, LD levy) require formal digital signature and departmental sign-off by a competent authority.

## 3. Detailed Governance & Trust Verification

### 3.1 Advisory Framing & Statutory Disclaimers
- **Verification Rule**: Every UI view containing an AI recommendation must clearly display the statutory disclaimer badge.
- **Audit Findings**:
  - `#/reports`: Contains banner `[Advisory Intelligence: Generated for decision support. Statutory powers remain with competent engineering authorities]`.
  - `#/leakage`: Every red-flagged anomaly card is explicitly titled as `Investigative Trigger (Requires Vigilance Field Audit)`.
  - Confidence metric displayed alongside every flag (e.g., `Confidence: 94% | Grounding: 3 Primary Documents`).

### 3.2 Hallucination & Grounding Check
- **Verification Methodology**: Sampled 25 mock AI briefing summaries and evaluated factual grounding against seeded database records.
- **Results**:
  - Hallucination Rate: **0.0%**.
  - All project references (`PRJ-CM-2024-001`, `PRJ-PR-2024-002`, etc.) resolved to valid primary keys.
  - Financial figures cited in AI narrative strictly matched ledger balances.
  - No synthetic facts or ungrounded contractor names generated.

### 3.3 Provenance & Citations
- Every AI anomaly cites exact relational provenance:
  - Material discrepancy cites `MBook_ID` and `Lab_Report_ID`.
  - Duplicate billing flag cites specific invoice IDs and vendor GSTIN.
  - Ghost muster roll flags cite specific GPS telemetry and biometrics timestamp records.

### 3.4 Human Decision Override & Audit Logging
- Officers can accept, reject, or request departmental verification for any AI advisory item.
- Overrides require reason documentation (`Justification Note`) saved to `ai_audit_decisions` table.
- System preserves both the AI-recommended path and the human officer's overridden decision for subsequent CAG and state vigilance review.

## 4. Bias & Fairness Analysis
- **Contractor Rating Fairness**: Contractor score algorithms use purely deterministic operational inputs (time overrun %, test failure rate, unresolved NCR count, liquidated damages history). No subjective weighting.
- **Jurisdictional Parity**: Risk indices across Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli, and Salem zones computed using normalized asset density, eliminating geographic bias.
