## 1. Verified Designation Catalogue & Service Cadre Structure

In strict compliance with service jurisprudence, engineering cadres must **never** be confused with political or Secretariat posts, and **Divisional Engineer (H)** must **never** be replaced with the generic central/PWD title "Executive Engineer".

### Statutory Committees & Decision Mandates:
1. **Board of Engineers (BOE)** `[VERIFIED OFFICIAL]`:
   - Highest Technical Authority; decides policy on engineering techniques, specifications, and administrative reforms.
2. **Technical Audit Committee (TAC)** `[VERIFIED OFFICIAL]`:
   - Constituted under Circular Memo No. 13555/Contracts-1/2009.
   - Mandated for bridge works with linear waterway &gt; 150m, major bypasses, grade separators, and ROBs/RUBs prior to Technical Sanction.
3. **Revised Administrative Sanction Committee (RASC)** `[VERIFIED OFFICIAL]`:
   - Constituted under G.O. Ms. No. 289 (04.12.2008) and G.O. Ms. No. 1 (07.01.2011).
   - Comprises DG (H), Concerned CE, CE (PDI), Deputy Secretary (Highways), and Deputy Secretary (Finance).
4. **RASC for Land Acquisition** `[VERIFIED OFFICIAL]`:
   - Constituted under G.O. Ms. No. 163 (13.12.2023).
   - Convener: Commissioner of Land Acquisition. Members: Finance Secretary Nominee, Additional Chief Secretary (Highways), Concerned CE, and Legal Affairs Secretary Representative.
5. **Commissionerate of Tenders (COT)** `[VERIFIED OFFICIAL]`:
   - Constituted under G.O. Ms. No. 21 (25.01.1999), G.O. Ms. No. 16 (11.02.2013), and G.O. Ms. No. 75 (02.09.2026).
   - Scrutinizes and approves tenders based on strict value and excess criteria:
     - Contract value up to ₹50 Lakhs with tender excess &gt; 10%
     - Contract value up to ₹1.00 Crore with tender excess &gt; 6%
     - Contract value between ₹1.00 Crore and ₹6.00 Crore for individual works (all cases with tender excess)
     - Contract value between ₹1.00 Crore and ₹7.50 Crore for packages (all cases with tender excess)
     - Contract value above ₹6.00 Crore for individual works (all cases irrespective of tender excess)
6. **Standard Schedule of Rates (SSR) Committee** `[VERIFIED OFFICIAL]`:
   - Prepares and updates annual departmental SSR; comprises DG (H), CE (C&M), CE (PDI), and Finance Department representative.
7. **High Level Dispute Resolution Committee (HLDRC)** `[VERIFIED OFFICIAL]`:
   - Constituted under G.O. Ms. No. 17 (06.03.2023) and G.O. Ms. No. 87 (21.07.2023) for amicable settlement of contractor claims and arbitration.
   - Chair: DG (H); Convener: Concerned CE; Members: Addl Chief Secretary (Finance), PD (TNRSP-II), Director (HRS), and Addl Chief Secretary (Law).
8. **Three Member Expert Committee under TNTIT Act Section 16(b)** `[VERIFIED OFFICIAL]`:
   - Reconstituted under G.O. Ms. No. 05 (13.01.2026).
   - Evaluates additional quantities exceeding 25% of agreement quantities, alternate items, and new items on single-source declaration.

## 5. Contractor & Consultant Account Architecture

Contractors and consultants are external entities and **must never be granted government cadre roles or departmental jurisdiction access**:

```
[ CONTRACTOR / CONSULTANT ACCOUNT BOUNDARY ]
  ├── Access strictly scoped to awarded contract packages (e.g., EPC01, EPC02)
  ├── Submissions: Milestone progress, R.A. Bills, As-Built drawings, Quality test certificates
  ├── Restrictions:
        ❌ Zero access to internal departmental disciplinary inquiries
        ❌ Zero access to confidential vigilance / financial leakage investigation indicators
        ❌ Zero access to High Court legal defense notes or internal AG audit correspondence
        ❌ Zero access to competitor contractor evaluations or peer bid performance
```

## 9. Historical Tenure, Acting Appointments & Transfer Preservation Model

In accordance with Government Service Rules:
1. When an officer is transferred or promoted, past signatures, measurement checks, and approvals are **permanently preserved** under their historical tenure record.
2. Temporary additional charge (`A/C`) and Full Additional Charge (`FAC`) appointments are distinguished from substantive regular postings.
3. Every appointment records `active_from`, `active_to`, `posting_type`, and the official Government Order reference.
