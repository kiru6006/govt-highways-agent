## 3. General Service & Ministerial Cadres in Highways Department

*Note: In accordance with Service Jurisprudence, administrative, legal, and accounts posts belong to the **Tamil Nadu General Service** or **Tamil Nadu Ministerial Service**, not the Highways Engineering cadre.*

| Statutory Post Title | Service Cadre | Administrative Role | Statutory Authority | Verification Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chief Personnel Officer (CPO)** | Tamil Nadu General Service | State-level establishment, promotions, service book audits under DG (H) | Non-technical administrative class | `[VERIFIED RULE]` |
| **Administrative Officer (AO)** | Tamil Nadu General Service | Office establishment in Chief Engineer and Circle offices | Ministerial promotion cadre | `[VERIFIED RULE]` |
| **Chief Accounts Officer (CAO)** | Tamil Nadu Treasuries & Accounts Service | Financial advice, IFMS LC reconciliation, pre-audit scrutiny in DG (H) office | Deputation from Treasuries & Accounts | `[VERIFIED RULE]` |
| **Accounts Officer (AO)** | Tamil Nadu Treasuries & Accounts Service | Bill scrutiny, contractor payment advice in Circle / Division offices | Deputation from Treasuries & Accounts | `[VERIFIED RULE]` |
