## 2. Multi-Assignment & Charge Type Legal Distinctions

In accordance with Fundamental Rules (FR 49) of the Tamil Nadu Government:
1. **Substantive Posting (`REGULAR`)**: The post to which an officer has been appointed following regular selection/promotion and for which they draw full substantive pay.
2. **Additional Charge (`A/C`)**: Temporary entrustment of the duties of an equivalent or higher vacant post in addition to the officer's own duties. An officer holding additional charge cannot exercise full statutory quasi-judicial powers of the post unless explicitly empowered.
   - *Example*: Er. S. Palanivel (CE Chennai) holding Additional Charge of CE Coimbatore.
   - *Example*: Er. S. Yamini (CE PDI) holding Additional Charge of CE TNRSP-II.
3. **Full Additional Charge (`FAC`)**: An administrative arrangement under FR 49 where an officer discharges all functions and statutory sign-offs of a vacant post until a regular incumbent joins.
   - *Example*: Thiru D. Baskara Pandian, IAS holding Full Additional Charge (`FAC`) of Project Director, CKICP.
